Agent Surfaces — Concept Feedback Tracker

What this is: six concepts for where Smart Window’s agents (Monitor · Scout · Research) should live in Firefox — each with a clickable storyboard and one shared live prototype. Try them, then leave feedback in the tables below, or use Docs comments on any line.

Live prototype — all six concepts on one engine; picking a concept in the top bar restarts it cold

How the prototypes work: every concept starts the same five ways (slash command, suggestion chips, just asking, Smart Window offering mid-chat, a URL-bar pill) and walks all three agents end to end — Monitor (watch until a condition is true), Scout (sweep right now, hand off to the Monitor; codes verified, never applied), Research (run once, keep a report). The concepts differ on one question only: where the watch lives and how it reaches you.

Concept A — All in the chat (violet)

Everything lives in the conversation. The watch is a live card in the thread (sparkline, stats, Edit/Pause/Delete), a pinned chip at the top of the panel expands to full detail, alerts land top-right and update the pin, and every output files as a child row under its chat in Firefox View → Chats. Cheapest build; the thread is the only record.

Concept B — A separate agents page (orange)

Creation in the chat with setup questions; the watch becomes a living pin up top and lands in the Agent Hub — a full page off the new tab with agent tiles, a reports library, and an activity log of every check. The new tab carries a summary card. The strongest management story and the most expensive single surface.

Concept C — A saved list in the panel, the Den (green)

Every chat keeps its own Den behind a Chat | Den toggle: only what this conversation created, suggestions fill it before anything starts (never empty), items are live with Edit/Pause/Delete, and alerts fire in the system corner. Panel-only by design — no cross-chat view.

Concept D — In the chat + the address bar (pink)

No list surface at all. The prefilled card stays in the chat; an always-there eye in the address bar carries a hover status card; when the condition hits, a floating action card finds you on any page (Open · Snooze · Stop). Reports open as real tabs you can bookmark or print.

Concept E — Dens in Firefox View (teal)

Creation in the chat; the live card keeps a permanent “Open in your Dens” link. The home is a Dens section in Firefox View beside Chats: big cards with the rule in plain words, a dot per check, Check now / Pause / Open page / Delete, and alert strips with the judgment. No address-bar indicator — one calm destination.
